Unit 1 · BIO1.LS1.1–1.7 · Q1 Wks 1–4How Can Bacterial Infections Make Us So Sick?
📌 TN 2022 Standards — Verified
LS1.1Compare & contrast characteristics of life. Argue whether viruses are non-living based on these characteristics.
LS1.2Evaluate comparative models of cell types with a focus on organic molecules that make up cellular structures.
LS1.3Develop a structural model of DNA; explain how DNA serves as a template for self-replication and encodes biological information.
LS1.4Demonstrate how DNA is decoded through transcription & translation to synthesize proteins; examine RNA structure & function.
LS1.5Research functional variety of proteins; plan and carry out a controlled investigation testing factors that affect protein structure & function.
LS1.7Model a cell plasma membrane; compare types of cellular transport; test predictions about homeostasis of energy and matter in cells.

Unit 2 · BIO1.LS1.6 · LS3.1–3 · ETS2.1–3 · Q1 Wks 5–9What Genetic Factors Contribute to Complex Disease?
📌 TN 2022 Standards — Verified
LS1.6Create a model for the major events of the eukaryotic cell cycle, including mitosis. Compare rates of cell division in various eukaryotic cell types.
LS3.1Model chromosome progression through meiosis and fertilization; compare and contrast sexual and asexual reproduction including advantages and disadvantages of each.
LS3.2Explain how protein formation results in phenotypic variation; discuss how changes in DNA lead to somatic or germ line mutations.
LS3.3Through pedigree analysis, identify patterns of trait inheritance; use mathematical thinking (Punnett squares) to predict likelihood of trait transmission.
ETS2.1Obtain, evaluate, and communicate information on how molecular biotechnology may be used in a variety of fields.
ETS2.2Investigate the means by which karyotypes are utilized in diagnostic medicine.
ETS2.3Analyze scientific and ethical arguments for pros and cons of a specific biotechnology technique (stem cells, IVF, GMOs).

Unit 3 · BIO1.LS1.8–9 · LS2.1–5 · Q2 Wks 10–14How Can We Improve a Food System?
📌 TN 2022 Standards — Verified
LS1.8Create a model of photosynthesis demonstrating the net flow of matter and energy into a cell; explain light energy conversion to stored chemical energy.
LS1.9Create a model of aerobic respiration demonstrating flow of matter and energy; compare aerobic respiration to anaerobic processes and fermentation.
LS2.1Analyze mathematical representations of population data; predict population size effects from ecosystem disturbances based on interdependent relationships.
LS2.2Create a model tracking carbon atoms between inorganic and organic molecules in an ecosystem; explain human impacts on climate based on this model.
LS2.3Analyze through research the cycling of matter in the biosphere; explain how biogeochemical cycles are critical for ecosystem function.
LS2.4Analyze data demonstrating decrease in biomass at successive trophic levels; represent energy and matter transfer in a mathematical model.
LS2.5Analyze examples of ecological succession; explain order of events responsible for new ecosystem formation following catastrophic disturbances.

Unit 4 · BIO1.LS4.1–3 · Q2 Wks 15–17Why Are So Many Species Declining — and Why Does It Matter?
📌 TN 2022 Standards — Verified
LS4.1Evaluate scientific data from molecular sequences, fossil records, biogeography, and embryology. Identify chronological patterns of change and communicate that biological evolution is supported by multiple lines of empirical evidence that identify homologies (common ancestry).
LS4.2Using a model that demonstrates change in allele frequencies resulting in evolution over many generations, identify causative agents of change (natural selection, genetic drift, gene flow, mutation).
LS4.3Identify ecosystem services and assess the role of biodiversity in support of these services. Analyze the role human activities have on disruption of these services.
⏱ Semester Note: Unit 4 is compressed (~15 days) due to the 1-semester schedule. BSCS UFL is designed as a full-year course. Prioritize LS4.2 (natural selection model) and LS4.3 (ecosystem services) as the highest-weight EOC standards in this domain.

⏱ Semester Compression Note: BSCS Understanding for Life is designed as a full-year program. Fitting all 4 units into 18 weeks with 90-minute blocks (~90 instructional days total) requires deliberate pacing. Unit 4 is the most compressed. Lean on the BSCS Flourish teacher pacing tools and prioritize the highest-weighted EOC standards within each unit when time is short.

For the Teacher🔗 How to Make Every Button Work

Every button showing PASTE-LINK-HERE needs a real URL. Here is exactly what each one needs and how to get it.

1
Course Syllabus
Upload your syllabus PDF to GitHub. Its URL is automatic once uploaded.
https://lhoward26.github.io/bio1-patriots/syllabus.pdf
⚙ Upload syllabus.pdf to GitHub → replace PASTE-SYLLABUS-LINK-HERE
2
Assignments Page, Grades, Discussions
In Canvas, navigate to each section. Copy the URL from your browser bar.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/assignments
⚙ Canvas → Assignments / Grades / Discussions → copy URL → paste into index.html
3
Open Module 1 / 2 / 3 / 4
In Canvas, go to Modules. Click the module title. Copy the URL — it ends in #module_XXXXX.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/modules#module_XXXXX
⚙ Get a separate URL for each of the 4 unit modules
4
Each Lesson / Lab / Activity Row
Build the Canvas item (Page, Assignment, or Quiz) first. Open it. Copy its URL. Each row needs its own unique link.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/pages/lesson-name
⚙ Build in Canvas → open → copy URL → find row in index.html → replace PASTE-LINK-HERE
5
Video Playlists (YouTube)
Create a YouTube playlist per unit. Open playlist → Share → Copy Link.
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
⚙ Do this for all 4 units separately
6
Quizlet Flashcard Sets
Create a Quizlet set per unit at quizlet.com. Open your set. Copy URL from browser.
https://quizlet.com/XXXXXXXXX/unit-1-biology-flash-cards/
⚙ Create 4 sets (one per unit) + 1 master vocabulary set for EOC
🔑 Golden Rule: Always build the Canvas item first, then come back and link it. Start with the 5 always-available links (syllabus, eBook, assignments, grades, discussion) in week 1, then add unit links as you build each module.
