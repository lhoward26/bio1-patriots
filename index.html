Unit 1 · BIO1.LS1.1–1.7Infectious Diseases
📌 TN 2022 Standards
BIO1.LS1.1Compare characteristics of life; argue whether viruses are living or non-living.
BIO1.LS1.2Evaluate models of cell types; focus on organic molecules that make up cellular structures.
BIO1.LS1.3Develop a structural model of DNA; explain self-replication and biological information storage.
BIO1.LS1.4Demonstrate how DNA is decoded through transcription & translation to synthesize proteins.
BIO1.LS1.5Research functional variety of proteins; plan an investigation testing factors that affect protein function.
BIO1.LS1.7Model a cell plasma membrane; compare types of transport; test predictions about homeostasis.

Unit 2 · BIO1.LS1.6 · LS3.1–3 · ETS2.1–3Heredity & Complex Diseases
📌 TN 2022 Standards
BIO1.LS1.6Model major events of the eukaryotic cell cycle including mitosis; compare rates of cell division.
BIO1.LS3.1Model chromosome progression through meiosis & fertilization; compare sexual vs. asexual reproduction.
BIO1.LS3.2Explain how protein formation results in phenotypic variation; discuss somatic vs. germ line mutations.
BIO1.LS3.3Use pedigree analysis to identify inheritance patterns; use Punnett squares to predict trait transmission.
BIO1.ETS2.1Obtain, evaluate, and communicate how molecular biotechnology is used across multiple fields.
BIO1.ETS2.2Investigate the means by which karyotypes are utilized in diagnostic medicine.
BIO1.ETS2.3Analyze scientific and ethical arguments surrounding biotechnology (stem cells, IVF, GMOs).

Unit 3 · BIO1.LS1.8–9 · LS2.1–5Sustainable Food Systems
📌 TN 2022 Standards
BIO1.LS1.8Model photosynthesis demonstrating net flow of matter and energy into a cell.
BIO1.LS1.9Model aerobic respiration; compare aerobic vs. anaerobic respiration and fermentation.
BIO1.LS2.1Analyze population data and carrying capacity; predict population size effects from ecosystem disturbances.
BIO1.LS2.2Model carbon atom movement between inorganic and organic molecules; explain human climate impacts.
BIO1.LS2.3Research how biogeochemical cycles (N, C, O, water) are critical for ecosystem function.
BIO1.LS2.4Analyze biomass decrease through trophic levels; model energy and matter transfer.
BIO1.LS2.5Analyze ecological succession; explain ecosystem formation following catastrophic disturbances.

Unit 4 · BIO1.LS4.1–3Protection of Biodiversity
📌 TN 2022 Standards
BIO1.LS4.1Evaluate scientific data from molecular sequences, fossil records, biogeography, and embryology as evidence for evolution and common ancestry.
BIO1.LS4.2Use a model demonstrating change in allele frequencies to show evolution of a population; identify causative agents (natural selection, genetic drift, gene flow, mutation).
BIO1.LS4.3Identify ecosystem services; assess the role of biodiversity in supporting those services; analyze human activities that disrupt them.

For the Teacher🔗 How to Make Every Button Work

Every button that says PASTE-LINK-HERE in the HTML code needs a real URL. Here is exactly what each button needs and how to get that URL.

1
Course Syllabus
Upload your syllabus PDF to GitHub first. Then its link is automatic.
https://lhoward26.github.io/bio1-patriots/syllabus.pdf
⚙ Upload syllabus.pdf to GitHub → replace PASTE-SYLLABUS-LINK-HERE with that URL
2
Digital Textbook (Flourish)
Already linked and working — no action needed.
https://www.flourishkh.com
⚙ Already set — students click and log in with their school credentials
3
Submit Work (Assignments Page)
In Canvas, go to your Assignments page. Copy the URL from your browser bar.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/assignments
⚙ Go to Canvas → Assignments → copy URL from browser → paste over PASTE-ASSIGNMENTS-PAGE-LINK-HERE
4
My Grades
In Canvas, click Grades in the left sidebar. Copy the URL from your browser.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/grades
⚙ Canvas → Grades → copy URL → paste over PASTE-GRADES-LINK-HERE
5
Open Module 1 / 2 / 3 / 4
In Canvas, go to Modules. Click the title of the module. Copy the URL — it ends in #module_XXXXX
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/modules#module_XXXXX
⚙ Do this separately for each of the 4 units — each gets its own module URL
6
Each Individual Lesson / Lab / Activity Row
Build the item in Canvas (Page, Assignment, or Quiz). Open it. Copy the URL from browser. Each lesson row in this page needs its own unique Canvas URL.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/pages/lesson-name
⚙ Build item in Canvas first → open it → copy URL → find that lesson row in index.html → replace PASTE-LINK-HERE
7
Video Playlists (YouTube)
Create a YouTube playlist for each unit. Open the playlist. Click Share → Copy Link.
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
⚙ YouTube → your playlist → Share → Copy Link → paste over PASTE-VIDEO-PLAYLIST-LINK-HERE (do for each unit)
8
Quizlet Flashcard Sets
Create a Quizlet set for each unit at quizlet.com. Open your set. Copy the URL from the browser.
https://quizlet.com/XXXXXXXXX/unit-1-biology-flash-cards/
⚙ Quizlet → your set → copy URL → paste over PASTE-QUIZLET-LINK-HERE (do for each unit)
9
IXL Practice
Already linked and working — no action needed.
https://www.ixl.com/science/skill-plans/tennessee-academic-standards-biology-i
⚙ Already set — direct link to TN Bio 1 skill plan on IXL
10
EOC Prep Quiz & Discussion Board
Build your EOC practice quiz in Canvas New Quizzes. Open it. Copy the URL. Same process for your Discussion board.
https://yourschool.instructure.com/courses/XXXXX/quizzes/XXXXX
⚙ Canvas → Quizzes → open quiz → copy URL → paste over PASTE-EOC-MODULE-LINK-HERE and PASTE-LINK-HERE spots
🔑 The Golden Rule: Always build the item in Canvas first, then come back and grab its link. You cannot link to something that doesn't exist yet. Work unit by unit — don't try to do everything at once. Start with the 6 quick links at the top (syllabus, eBook, assignments, grades) since those are always available from day one.
